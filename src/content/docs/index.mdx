# Acorn TV Login: How to Sign In on Any Device


Acorn TV lets you stream British mysteries, dramas, and comedies without a cable box — but the login step trips up a surprising number of people, especially on streaming devices where typing a password with a remote is painful. This guide walks through the exact sign-in steps for every supported device, plus fixes for the login errors people hit most.

## Quick Answer

Go to acorn.tv or open the Acorn TV app, tap **Sign In**, and enter the email and password you used when you subscribed. On a TV or streaming device, use the on-screen **activation code** instead of typing your password with a remote — it's faster and less error-prone.

## Acorn TV Login by Device

### Website (Desktop or Laptop)

1. Go to the official Acorn TV website.
2. Select **Sign In** in the top-right corner.
3. Enter the email tied to your Acorn TV account.
4. Enter your password.
5. Select **Sign In** to load your library.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot Password** on the same screen — [full steps below](#acorn-tv-forgot-password).

### Mobile App (iPhone or Android)

1. Install the Acorn TV app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Launch the app and tap **Sign In** on the opening screen.
3. Enter your account email and password.
4. Tap **Sign In** to confirm.

Turn on **Stay Signed In** ("Remember Me" on some versions) so you're not re-entering credentials every session.

### Roku

1. Open the Acorn TV app on your Roku — a short activation code appears on screen.
2. On your phone or computer, go to Acorn TV's activation page.
3. Sign in with your email and password if prompted.
4. Enter the Roku activation code.
5. Your Roku signs in automatically.

Prefer typing directly? Roku's on-screen keyboard also accepts email/password entry.

### Fire TV (Firestick)

Same flow as Roku: open the Acorn TV app, note the activation code, then enter it on Acorn TV's activation page from your phone or computer. This is the recommended method on Firestick — the Fire TV remote's text entry is slow.

### Apple TV

Apple TV supports manual sign-in and the activation code method. Given the remote's text entry, most users find the activation code faster: open the app, grab the code, enter it via browser on your phone or computer.

### Chromecast and Android TV

Same activation-code pattern: launch the app, note the code, activate it at acorn.tv/activate on a second device.

| Method | Best for | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| Manual entry | Phone, computer | Slow, error-prone on a TV remote |
| Activation code | Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Chromecast, Android TV | Code expires after a few minutes |

### Signing In Through Amazon Prime Video Channels

If you subscribed to Acorn TV as an add-on through Amazon Prime Video Channels rather than directly through acorn.tv, sign in with your Amazon account inside the Prime Video app instead — Acorn TV's own login screen won't recognize that subscription.

## Acorn TV Login Problems and How to Fix Them

### Acorn TV Forgot Password

Select **Forgot Password**, enter your account email, and Acorn TV emails a reset link. Check spam/promotions if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes — the link expires after a set window, so use it right away.

### Acorn TV Account Not Found

Almost always a wrong-email issue. Check for:

- A typo in the domain (".con" instead of ".com")
- A leading/trailing space pasted in from elsewhere
- An old email address you no longer use

Not sure which address you used? Search your inbox for past Acorn TV billing or welcome emails.

### App Frozen on the Login Screen

1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart the device (phone, TV, or streaming box).
3. Confirm your internet connection works — test another app or site.
4. Update Acorn TV to the latest version.
5. Clear the app's cache (Android: **Settings > Apps > Acorn TV > Storage > Clear Cache**).

### Activation Code Not Working

Codes are only valid a few minutes. Back out of the activation flow, reopen the app on your TV to generate a fresh code, and repeat the steps immediately.

### "Too Many Devices" / Keeps Logging Me Out

Your plan caps simultaneous streams. Sign out of the app on devices you're not using, or manage active sessions from your account settings on the website.

### Signed In but Can't Watch Anything

Usually a billing issue, not a login issue. A failed payment can leave you signed in but blocked at the content level. Check your payment method under account settings.

### Won't Let Me Log In (Browser Issues)

- Clear cookies/cache for the site, or try a different browser.
- Temporarily disable ad blockers or privacy extensions — they sometimes interfere with the login form.

### Two-Factor Authentication Code Never Arrives

Check spam, confirm your phone has signal, and request a new code — old codes expire and won't work even if they arrive late.

## FAQ

**Can I use Acorn TV on more than one device at once?**
Yes, up to the simultaneous-stream limit on your plan.

**Do I need a new account for each device?**
No — one account works across web, mobile, and TV apps.

**What's faster: typing my password on a TV remote or using the activation code?**
The activation code, in almost every case — it skips remote-based typing entirely.

**Why does my activation code keep expiring?**
Codes are time-limited by design for security. Generate a new one right before use rather than in advance.

## Bottom Line

Most Acorn TV sign-in issues trace back to one of three things: a mistyped email, an expired reset/activation link, or an outdated app version. The checklist above resolves most login problems without contacting support — but if you're still stuck, Acorn TV's support team can look at your account directly.
