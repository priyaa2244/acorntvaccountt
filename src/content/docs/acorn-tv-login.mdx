# Acorn TV Login: How to Sign In on Any Device

Acorn TV lets you stream British mysteries, dramas, and comedies without a cable box, but a lot of first-time (and returning) users get stuck at the Acorn TV login screen. This guide covers the exact acorn.tv login steps for every device Acorn TV supports, plus fixes for the login problems people run into most often.

## Quick Answer

To complete an Acorn TV login, go to acorn.tv or open the app, tap **Sign In**, and enter the email and password you used when you subscribed. On a TV or streaming device, the Acorn TV activation code is usually faster than typing your password with a remote.

## Acorn TV Login by Device

### Acorn TV Login on the Website (Desktop or Laptop)

1. Go to the official Acorn TV website.
2. Select **Sign In** in the top-right corner.
3. Type the email tied to your Acorn TV account.
4. Enter your password.
5. Select **Sign In** to load your library.

Forgot your Acorn TV password? Use the **Forgot Password** link on the same screen — it's covered in more detail below.

### Acorn TV Login on the Mobile App (iPhone or Android)

1. Install the Acorn TV app from the App Store or Google Play if it isn't already on your phone.
2. Launch the app and tap **Sign In** on the opening screen.
3. Enter your account email and password.
4. Tap **Sign In** again to confirm.

Turning on **Stay Signed In** (sometimes labeled "Remember Me") keeps you logged in between sessions, so you're not retyping credentials every time you open the app.

### Acorn TV Login on Roku

1. Open the Acorn TV app on your Roku — a short activation code appears on screen.
2. On your phone or computer, go to Acorn TV's activation page.
3. Sign in with your email and password if prompted.
4. Enter the Roku activation code.
5. Your Roku signs in automatically — no need to type your password with the remote.

If you'd rather not use the activation flow, you can also type your email and password directly using Roku's on-screen keyboard.

### Acorn TV Login on Fire TV (Firestick)

The Fire TV login works the same way as Roku: open the Acorn TV app, note the activation code, then enter it at Acorn TV's activation page on a second device. This is the recommended method for Firestick, since typing a password with the Fire TV remote is slow and easy to fumble.

### Acorn TV Login on Apple TV

Apple TV supports both manual sign-in and the activation code method. Given Apple TV's remote-based text entry, most users find the activation code noticeably faster — open the app, grab the code, and enter it through a browser on your phone or computer.

### Acorn TV Login on Chromecast and Android TV

These platforms follow the same activation code pattern as Roku and Fire TV: launch the app, note the code shown on screen, then activate it from acorn.tv/activate on another device.

| Method | Best for | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| Manual entry | Devices with easy text input (phone, computer) | Slow, error-prone on a TV remote |
| Activation code | Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Chromecast, Android TV | Code expires after a few minutes |

### Acorn TV Login Through Amazon Prime Video Channels

If you subscribed to Acorn TV as an add-on through Amazon Prime Video Channels rather than directly through acorn.tv, sign in with your Amazon account inside the Prime Video app instead — Acorn TV's own login screen won't recognize that subscription.

## Acorn TV Login Problems and How to Fix Them

### Acorn TV Forgot Password

Select **Forgot Password**, enter your account email, and Acorn TV sends a reset link. Check spam or promotions folders if it doesn't show up within a few minutes — the link typically expires after a set window, so use it promptly.

### Acorn TV Account Not Found

This almost always means the wrong email is being entered. Check for:

- A typo in the domain (".con" instead of ".com" is a common one)
- A leading or trailing space copied in from elsewhere
- An old email address you no longer use

If you're not sure which address you signed up with, search your inbox for past Acorn TV billing or welcome emails — they'll show the account email.

### Acorn TV App Frozen on the Login Screen

Work through these in order:

1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart the device itself (phone, TV, or streaming box).
3. Confirm your internet connection is actually working — try another app or site.
4. Update Acorn TV to the latest version.
5. Clear the app's cache (Android: Settings > Apps > Acorn TV > Storage > Clear Cache).

### Acorn TV Activation Code Not Working

Activation codes are only valid for a few minutes. Back out of the activation flow, reopen the Acorn TV app on your TV to generate a fresh code, and repeat the activation steps right away.

### Acorn TV Too Many Devices / Keeps Logging Me Out

Your plan caps how many simultaneous streams you get. If you're locked out, sign out of the app on devices you're not actively using — you can also manage active sessions from your account settings on the website.

### Signed In to Acorn TV but Can't Watch Anything

This is usually a billing issue, not a login issue. If a payment failed, you'll often still be able to sign in but get blocked at the content itself. Check your payment method under account settings and update it if needed.

### Acorn TV Won't Let Me Log In (Browser Issues)

- Clear cookies and cache for the site, or try a different browser entirely.
- Disable ad blockers or privacy extensions temporarily — they sometimes interfere with the login form.

### Acorn TV Two-Factor Authentication Code Never Arrives

Check spam folders, confirm your phone has signal, and request a new code — old codes expire quickly and won't work even if they finally arrive.

## FAQ

**Can I use Acorn TV on more than one device at once?**
Yes, up to the simultaneous-stream limit on your specific plan.

**Do I need a new account for each device?**
No — one Acorn TV account and login work across web, mobile, and TV apps.

**What's faster: typing my password on a TV remote or using the activation code?**
The activation code, in almost every case — it avoids remote-based typing entirely.

**Why does my activation code keep expiring?**
Codes are time-limited by design for security. Generate a new one right before you use it rather than in advance.

## Bottom Line

Most Acorn TV sign-in issues trace back to one of three things: a mistyped email, an expired reset or activation link, or an outdated app version. Working through the checklist above resolves the large majority of login problems without needing to contact support — but if you're still stuck, Acorn TV's customer support team can look at your account directly.
